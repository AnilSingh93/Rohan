GOOD TO KNOW

Questions on your mind?

Here are a few things students often ask before joining us.

Still have questions? Talk to us →
Do you organise yoga retreats as well as teacher training courses?

Yes. We offer yoga retreats combining yoga, meditation, relaxation and cultural experiences for anyone seeking rest or a deeper connection with yoga.

How can I choose the right course?

The best course depends on your goals, previous experience and available time. Our team can help you compare programs and recommend a suitable option.

What is the difference between 100, 200 and 300 hour courses?

The 100-hour course is a foundation program. The 200-hour course is the internationally recognised starting point for teaching, while the 300-hour course is advanced training for graduates of a 200-hour program.

What should I bring with me?

Bring comfortable practice clothing, a warm layer, toiletries and personal essentials. Yoga materials and props are provided by the school.

Is Rishikesh safe for solo travellers?

Many students travel independently. Our residential setting offers a settled campus and a supportive group environment. Normal travel awareness is always recommended.
